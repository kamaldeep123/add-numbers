Solution 2: Fix EmailJS Gmail Integration

Resolve the Gmail API scope issue with these steps:

Step 1: Re-authenticate Gmail Service

  • Go to your EmailJS Dashboard
  • Click on "Email Services"
  • Find your Gmail service and click "Edit"
  • Click "Reconnect Account"
  • Important: Make sure to grant ALL requested permissions

Step 2: Check Required Scopes

Ensure your Gmail service has these scopes enabled:

✅ https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send ✅ https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.compose ✅ https://mail.google.com/ (Full access)

Step 3: Test Email Service

  • In EmailJS dashboard, go to your Gmail service
  • Click "Send Test Email"
  • If it fails, repeat Step 1 with careful attention to permissions
Note: If you're still getting scope errors, Gmail might have restricted your app. You may need to use an alternative email service or wait 24-48 hours before retrying.

Solution 3: Use Alternative Email Service in EmailJS

Switch to a different email provider that's more reliable:

Option A: Use Outlook/Hotmail

  • In EmailJS, add "Outlook" service
  • Connect with a Microsoft account
  • Usually has fewer authentication issues

Option B: Use SMTP Service

  • Add "Custom SMTP" service in EmailJS
  • Use any email provider's SMTP settings
  • More reliable but requires SMTP configuration
